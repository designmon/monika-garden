# Interesting Takes Engine

I am the only woman on a design team working on AI for Miro, and I often feel behind on what is happening. Other people, mostly guys, seem to have hot takes quickly and say them with a lot of confidence, even when the take is temporary. I notice a lot of their signal comes from X, interesting industry people, sharp blogs, academic work, labs, and whatever suddenly matters this week.

I want something that helps me understand new AI/design ideas as they arrive: why they are big right now, what the underlying shift is, what is hype, what is real, and what older ideas they echo. It should give me simple metaphors tied to daily life, not only computer life, so I can actually feel the idea and explain it without sounding like I memorized a thread.

Over time, the takes should be shaped by my values. I do not just want louder opinions; I want clearer, grounded, interesting thoughts that feel like mine. It should connect current signals from academia, leading AI labs, industry writing, and older thinkers, because many "new" ideas are old questions returning in new clothes.

## Bloom looks like

A companion that turns current AI/design discourse into digestible briefings, metaphors, questions, and opinion seeds I can use in conversation and at work, with a voice that grows closer to my values over time.
