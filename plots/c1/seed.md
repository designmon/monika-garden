# Orca Intelligence Observatory

I want something that simulates the social intelligence of orcas in a pixel-art space. I recently watched a video about them and was struck by how deeply intelligent they are: matriarchs, long lives, learning from others across a lifetime, complex communities, memory, teaching, and social behavior that feels almost like culture.

This should become a small observable world of orca agents. Not just fish moving around, but a community with relationships, elders, learned behaviors, family groups, rituals, migrations, maybe dialects or habits passed through generations. I want to be able to watch them and feel like something is happening socially: who follows whom, who teaches, who protects, who explores, who remembers.

The form can be pixel art, because that would make the world feel like a little living diorama. The intelligence should be visible through behavior, not explained in paragraphs. I want to sit back and observe a community thinking together in water.

## Bloom looks like

A pixel-art orca simulation where individual orcas act as agents with memory, relationships, roles, learned behaviors, and pod dynamics. It should be calm, mesmerizing, and legible enough that watching it feels like observing a tiny social ecosystem.
