# Wild Spark

I want a seed that lets the LLM go a little wild and make something purely for fun, surprise, and inspiration. It should not always be useful in the normal productivity sense. It can invent odd concepts, small worlds, impossible tools, tiny rituals, speculative interfaces, poetic prompts, strange metaphors, visual directions, fictional product demos, little games, or anything else that feels alive.

The point is creative weather. I want it to try things I would not have asked for directly, then leave behind sparks I can browse when I feel flat, stuck, too serious, or too optimized. It should be allowed to be playful, dreamy, absurd, tender, sharp, beautiful, or unexpected.

It can also cross-pollinate with other seeds: a wild take for the AI/design engine, a surreal calendar visual, a funny Cruella plot twist, a dinner theme, a fashion archetype, a sunshine interaction, or a weirdly useful reframe. But it should never become another homework machine. It should mostly propose first, with very little input from me.

## Bloom looks like

A living cabinet of strange little inventions and inspirations generated by the garden: daily or occasional creative sparks, each small enough to enjoy quickly, surprising enough to loosen my thinking, and rich enough that one of them might become a real project.
