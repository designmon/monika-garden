# Dinner Circle

I want something that helps me regularly host dinners, maybe every two months. I like the idea from the screenshot: six dinner parties a year, always eight people, always a mix of people from different parts of life. It does not have to follow that exact formula, but I want to explore the spirit of it: taking gathering seriously, planning it like a project, and making connection something I actively create.

The thing should make hosting easier for me. It could help choose dates, remember who I have not seen in a while, suggest a good mix of guests, draft invitations, plan a simple menu, track dietary needs, suggest conversation prompts, and reduce the emotional friction of "this is too much work." I want it to make the dinner feel intentional without making it stiff.

The heart of it is not optimization. It is helping me build a rhythm of bringing people together: someone I have known for years, someone new, someone from work, someone from another part of my life, people who might not otherwise meet. It should support warmth, generosity, atmosphere, and the tiny logistics that make hospitality actually happen.

## Bloom looks like

A small dinner-hosting companion that helps me plan a recurring dinner every two months: guest mix, invitations, menu, reminders, table notes, and maybe a memory archive of each evening so the practice becomes part of my life.
