# Style Atlas

I want something that helps me develop my fashion style based on famous people whose style I like. I would tell it whose style I am drawn to, and it would help me understand the ingredients: silhouettes, colors, fabrics, attitude, styling tricks, recurring pieces, and what makes the look work.

Then I want it to help me find affordable items from accessible stores that move me toward that style, with a strong preference for natural materials. I try not to buy polyester, so fabric quality and material filters matter. It should help me build taste without just copying, and shop with more intention.

## Bloom looks like

A style guide and shopping helper that translates admired references into practical outfits and affordable natural-material pieces, while preserving my own taste and constraints.
