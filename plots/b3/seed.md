# Visual Voice Calendar

I want a visual journal where I can record daily thoughts and observations as voice notes, and have them become a beautiful visual calendar. Each day should feel like a small story or artifact, not just a transcript.

I also want to explore dynamic and generative interfaces here. The calendar's visual language should adapt based on my mood: maybe color, density, texture, composition, rhythm, or symbolic motifs shift as the days accumulate. I am drawn to Pinterest-like visual calendars and diary layouts, but I want this to become more alive and personal than a static template.

The interface itself is part of the exploration: how can an interface generate itself around memory, mood, and voice? How can it make a month feel like a visual landscape of my inner weather?

## Bloom looks like

A prototype where voice notes become daily visual entries, and the month view gradually develops a generative visual language shaped by mood, themes, and personal observations.
